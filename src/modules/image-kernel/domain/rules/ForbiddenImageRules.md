# Forbidden Image Rules

The live source of truth is `MASTER_IMAGE_CREATION_RULES.md`.

Hard defaults:
- No empty offices or other empty professional environments.
- No environment dominance.
- No passive decorative humans.
- No generic corporate stock scenes.
- No keyword clichés such as robots for AI, money piles for finance, containers for trade, or hooded hackers for security.
- No distorted anatomy, fake text, watermarks, duplicated people, incorrect equipment, or visual clutter.
