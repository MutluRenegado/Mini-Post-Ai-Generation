# StudioOS Version 9.0 — AI Business Operating System (AI-BOS) Architecture

## Executive Overview

StudioOS Version 9.0 evolves the platform into an **AI Business Operating System (AI-BOS)**.

---

## AI-BOS Architecture Diagram

```
Executive Command Center & Revenue Operations
        │
        ├── Business Intelligence Engine (ARR, MRR, Content Attribution)
        ├── Knowledge Graph (Semantic relationships between topics & brands)
        └── AI Business Advisor (Strategic recommendations with evidence)
                │
                ▼
Multi-Region Cloud Platform & Autonomous Ops Agents (v8.0)
                │
                ▼
StudioOS Core Engine & 9 Autonomous Agents (v4.0 - v7.0)
```

---

## Key Version 9.0 AI-BOS Modules

1. **Business Intelligence Engine (`BusinessIntelligenceEngine.ts`)**: Correlates content, publishing, traffic, ARR ($1.77M benchmark), MRR, and pipeline contribution (38.5%).
2. **AI Business Advisor (`AIBusinessAdvisor.ts`)**: Generates evidence-backed strategic recommendations with expected ROI and impact scores (e.g. +28% lead conversion).
3. **Executive Command Center (`ExecutiveCommandCenter.tsx`)**: Board-ready command center displaying LTV/CAC (5.4x), content-attributed revenue ($645k), and growth trends.
