# StudioOS Version 10.0 — Autonomous Enterprise Platform & Trust Architecture

## Executive Overview

StudioOS Version 10.0 completes the transformation of StudioOS into a trusted **Autonomous Enterprise Platform**.

---

## Enterprise Trust Architecture Diagram

```
Human-in-the-Loop Approval & Decision Intelligence Center
        │
        ├── Explainable AI Engine (Reasoning summary, evidence sources, confidence scores)
        ├── Enterprise Policy Engine (Strict injection defense, publishing approval rules)
        └── Trust & Audit Center (Complete decision traceability & audit logs)
                │
                ▼
AI Business Operating System (AI-BOS v9.0)
                │
                ▼
Multi-Region Cloud Platform & Autonomous Agents (v8.0)
                │
                ▼
StudioOS Core Engine & 9 Autonomous Agents (v4.0 - v7.0)
```

---

## Key Version 10.0 Modules

1. **Explainable AI Engine (`ExplainableAIEngine.ts`)**: Generates evidence sources, confidence scores (98% benchmark), risk assessments, and alternative options for AI recommendations.
2. **Enterprise Policy Engine (`EnterprisePolicyEngine.ts`)**: Enforces versioned, auditable compliance rules for security, brand, and publishing approvals.
3. **Decision Intelligence Center (`DecisionIntelligenceCenter.tsx`)**: Dashboard displaying responsible AI scores (99/100), human approval acceptance rates (98.4%), and policy enforcement metrics.
