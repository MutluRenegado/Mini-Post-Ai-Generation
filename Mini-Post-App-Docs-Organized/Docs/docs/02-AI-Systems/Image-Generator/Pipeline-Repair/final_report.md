## 2. Root Cause & Solution Summary

- **Previous Flaw**: Image generation could be triggered early from raw topics or unapproved post drafts, leading to abstract letter artifacts (e.g. rendering giant letters for "Letter of Credit").
- **Enforced Solution**:
  1. **Final Text Gate**: Blocked image generation unless `textStatus === 'FINAL'` or `'APPROVED'`.
  2. **Summarizer & Concept Resolver**: Added `FinalTextVisualSummarizer` and `VisualConceptResolver` to summarize complete final text and convert abstract concepts into real-world business scenes.
  3. **Prompt & Validation**: `ImagePromptBuilder` applies strong negative constraints (`no text`, `no letters`, `no logos`, `no watermarks`), verified by `ImagePromptValidator` before provider dispatch.
