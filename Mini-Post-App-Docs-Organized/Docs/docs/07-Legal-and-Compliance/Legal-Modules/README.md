## 1. Module Purpose & Scope

The **Legal Module** serves as the centralized single source of truth for all legal contracts, privacy disclosures, terms of service, cookie policies, GDPR data rights, subprocessors, disclaimers, data retention rules, and legal navigation across Mini Post App.

### Core Governance Principles
- **Data Deletion Protection**: `/data-deletion` is preserved as an active, stateful account erasure and token revocation workflow.
- **Draft & Review Status**: All legal content displays `DRAFT FOR REVIEW - PENDING LEGAL APPROVAL` until reviewed by legal counsel.
- **Fact Accuracy**: Built using strictly verified corporate details (`Yoga Products Top Limited`, `minipostapp.space`, `support@minipostapp.space`).
